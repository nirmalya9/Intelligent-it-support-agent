# VPN connection and authentication troubleshooting

Symptoms: VPN authentication failures, frequent disconnects, or inability to reach internal sites.

1. Confirm the device has a working internet connection before starting the VPN.
2. Check that the username is correct and that the account is not locked.
3. After a password change, sign out of the VPN client and enter the updated credentials. Never share passwords or MFA codes with support.
4. Confirm the device clock is correct because large clock differences can affect authentication.
5. If the VPN connects but internal names do not resolve, record the error and contact network support.
6. If the issue persists, attach the exact error message and approximate time of failure.
Escalate if several users are affected or approved troubleshooting steps fail.
